UCI Research Assistant — ScaleSense / Autonomous Driving Perception
University of California, Irvine | Dec. 2025–Present

Conducting research on multimodal autonomous-driving perception using CARLA, SUMO, radar, cameras, Python, PyTorch, and Linux. Built an end-to-end simulation and dataset-generation framework for evaluating infrastructure-mounted radar-camera configurations under controlled traffic scenarios.

Developed configurable 4-, 8-, 12-, and 14-radar layouts and synchronized radar detections, camera frames, vehicle metadata, 3D bounding boxes, and sensor calibration information through CARLA's synchronous simulation environment. Built offline labeling and QA pipelines to associate radar returns with simulated actors, analyze sensor coverage and point density, and identify labeling or dataset-quality failures without rerunning simulations.

Used the generated datasets to evaluate radar-based perception approaches including PointNet and RadarGNN, giving the research pipeline coverage from simulation and data collection through model training and evaluation.
